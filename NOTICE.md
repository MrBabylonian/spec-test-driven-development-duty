The package layout and test-driven methodology were informed by [`obra/superpowers`](https://github.com/obra/superpowers), which is distributed under the MIT License. `vendor/test-driven-development/SKILL.md` is a verbatim copy of the user's selected installed skill, version `1.1.0`, authored as `Hermes Agent (adapted from obra/superpowers)` under the MIT License. Its source digest and identity are recorded in `COMPONENTS.json`; the portable wrapper under `skills/` loads that exact file without altering it.

`vendor/simplify-code/SKILL.md` is a verbatim copy of the installed `software-development/simplify-code` skill, version `1.1.0`. `vendor/requesting-code-review/` is a verbatim copy of the selected root `requesting-code-review` skill and its required reviewer template. Their source identities and digests are recorded in `COMPONENTS.json`; portable wrappers load the exact vendored files.

`vendor/subagent-driven-development/` is a verbatim copy of the installed `software-development/subagent-driven-development` skill, version `1.1.0`, and both of its references. Its source identity and file digests are recorded in `COMPONENTS.json`; the portable wrapper loads that exact source.
